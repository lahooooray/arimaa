### **ARIMAA**  
Arimaa is an interesting strategic board game for two players, somewhat resembling chess.  

### **Objective of the game:**  
Move one of your rabbits to the opposite side of the board.  

### **Player's pieces:**  
Each player has 16 pieces:  
- **1 Elephant**  
- **1 Camel**  
- **2 Horses**  
- **2 Dogs**  
- **2 Cats**  
- **8 Rabbits**  

The pieces are listed in order from strongest to weakest.  

### **Game board:**  
- An **8x8 square grid**, similar to a chessboard.  
- Rows are numbered **1 to 8**, and columns are labeled with **letters A to H** (from left to right).  
- The board has **4 "trap" squares**—when a piece steps on one, it may be removed from the game **unless there is a friendly piece adjacent to it**.  

### **Controls:**  
- **Controlled using a computer mouse.**  
- To move a piece, the player **first clicks on the piece** and then **on the square** where they want to move it.  

### **Game Modes:**  
Before starting, the player can choose between:  
- **Player vs. Player**  
- **Player vs. Computer**  

---

### **Piece Placement:**  
Pieces can be placed in two ways:  
1. **"DEFAULT PLACEMENT" button** – Pieces are placed automatically.  
2. **Manually** – The player places each piece in any order, or places some manually and then clicks **"DEFAULT PLACEMENT"** to place the remaining pieces on the board.  

---

### **Movement:**  
- Pieces can move **one square** in any direction **except the rabbits**, which **cannot move backward**.  
- Each turn, a player can make **1 to 4 moves** with any combination of their pieces.  
- **Pushing an opponent’s piece:** A weaker enemy piece is moved to an empty square, while the stronger piece that pushed it moves into the vacated square.  
- **Pulling an opponent’s piece:** The player's piece moves first, and the opponent's piece moves into the player's previous position.  

**NOTES:**  
- A piece can only push/pull another if it is **stronger**.  
- A piece **cannot push or pull if it is frozen**.  
- **Pushing and pulling each count as 2 moves.**  

---

### **Buttons:**  
1. **PULL and PUSH** – Special moves for pulling and pushing an opponent’s piece.  
   - First, select **two pieces**:  
     - Your piece (the one that will push or pull).  
     - The opponent’s piece (the one being pushed or pulled).  
   - Then, click on the square where you want to move:  
     - **For pull:** Select where to move your piece.  
     - **For push:** Select where to move the opponent’s piece.  

2. **FINISH MOVE** – Allows the player to **end their turn** without using all 4 moves.  

3. **RESIGN** – The player **immediately loses** the game.  

---

### **End of the Game:**  
A player **loses** if:  
- They click **"RESIGN"**.  
- They lose **all of their rabbits**.  
- They **cannot make a legal move**.  
- The opponent’s rabbit **reaches the opposite side of the board**.  

After the game ends, players can:  
- **Save** the game move by move according to the official game notation.
